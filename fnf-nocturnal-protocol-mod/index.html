A Dark Rhythm Battle From Tubbyland

FNF Nocturnal Protocol Mod pulls players into a tense Friday Night Funkin’ battle inspired by the eerie world of Slendytubbies and Myths of Tubbyland. Instead of a bright and silly rap duel, this mod builds its mood through heavy music, dark visuals, and a mysterious opponent known as the Guardian. The result is a rhythm experience that feels slow, suspenseful, and intense from the first beat.

The mod focuses on recreating the Nocturnal Protocol song, originally connected to the larger Myths of Tubbyland project. Fans of Slendytubbies may notice the darker tone, the white teletubby figure, and the strange atmosphere that makes every note feel more serious. Even players who do not know the source material can still enjoy the strong soundtrack and focused rhythm challenge.

Face The Guardian In A Haunting Track

The main opponent in FNF Nocturnal Protocol Mod is the Guardian, a white teletubby figure holding a flashlight. His appearance gives the battle a horror-inspired feeling, while his calm but unsettling presence makes the stage feel different from most FNF mods.

The song uses deep sounds, tense pacing, and a memorable whistle reference that adds to the creepy atmosphere. There are no overly complex extra mechanics here. The challenge comes from reading the notes, staying focused, and keeping control while the visuals try to pull attention away from the chart.

Multiple Endings Add Replay Value

One interesting part of the mod is its multiple endings. The result can change depending on how well the player performs during the match. A clean run may lead to a different ending than a messy performance, which gives players a reason to try again and improve their timing.

This makes FNF Nocturnal Protocol Mod more than a one-time song. It becomes a short but replayable rhythm challenge for players who enjoy unlocking every possible outcome.

How To Play FNF Nocturnal Protocol Mod

Press the notes when they match the symbols on screen. Good timing keeps the health bar steady, while too many missed notes can end the song early.

Controls

  • Arrow Keys or WASD: Hit notes
  • Enter: Select menu options
  • Esc: Pause or exit
  • Space: Respond to prompts
